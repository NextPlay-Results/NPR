01

Demand Letter Drafting

After writing over a thousand demand letters, we know what gets responses and what ends up in the “nice try” pile. You’ll get case law that actually applies to your venue, damage calculations that hold up, and language that makes adjusters think twice before lowballing.

  • → Real case law research, not copy-paste from templates
  • → Damages calculations you can support at deposition, mediation/arbitration, or trial.
  • → Tone calibrated to your specific style of writing. (Don’t worry; we acknowledge that no two attorneys practice law the same way.)
  • → Written by a human who has been negotiating legal settlements for a long time (or as our kids say, “they were born in the 1900s.”)
02

Medical Chronology

We've organized medical records for hundreds of cases that actually went to trial. That means we don't just list dates. We spot the gaps, flag the credibility issues, and set up your causation timeline so your expert can walk right into their testimony.

  • → Organized for cross-examination, not just chronological order
  • → Provider background checks and credential verification
  • → Causation markers highlighted for expert handoff
  • → Missing records and timeline gaps called out upfront
03

Pleadings, Motions & Discovery

Twenty years drafting the documents that move a case — complaints and answers, motions that get granted, and discovery that actually develops the record. We draft pleadings, motions, discovery requests, and discovery responses tuned to your venue's rules and judge's preferences, ready for your review and signature.

  • → Complaints, answers, and pleadings drafted to local rules
  • → Motions with supporting points and authorities that hold up
  • → Discovery requests — interrogatories, RFPs, RFAs — targeted to your theory
  • → Discovery responses and objections, properly preserved
04

Tort Claim Notices

Experience preparing and serving Tort Claims Act notices and ensuring compliance with statutory pre-suit requirements. Naming a public entity means tighter deadlines, immunity carve-outs, and procedural traps that kill cases before they’re filed. We’ve cleared all three.

  • → State-specific Tort Claims Act compliance and deadlines
  • → Proper agency identification and service of notice
  • → Statutory pre-suit requirements documented and tracked
  • → Damage itemization formatted to statute requirements
05

Case Management Setup

Filevine Master Certified and a Clio Certified Administrator. We set up, migrate, and optimize your case management system so your matters, deadlines, documents, and billing actually run the way the software promised — not the half-configured mess most firms live with.

  • → Filevine and Clio setup, migration, and cleanup
  • → Matter templates, workflows, and deadline automation built to your practice
  • → Document management and intake forms wired to your process
  • → Team training so it sticks after we hand it off
06

Legal AI Coaching

Your associates are already using AI. They just haven’t told you. Let’s get everyone on the same workflow — tools that work, prompts that don’t hallucinate, and a process that won’t end up in a sanctions order.

  • → Setup that respects privilege (still matters, somehow people forget)

  • → Training your team will actually use — not another LinkedIn Learning course
