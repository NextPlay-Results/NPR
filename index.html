Who actually does the work?

Me. Licensed, twenty years in the trenches. Mostly PI, med mal, and workers comp, but I've also done corporate, probate, and insurance defense work. I've written over 1,000 demand letters, prepped for 250+ trials, and sat through 20 of them in court. Every project gets my eyes on it before it goes out.

Do you just use ChatGPT or AI templates?

Absolutely not. I use AI to speed up research and initial drafts, but then I actually read and rewrite everything. You're not getting some ChatGPT output with my name slapped on it. If a citation's in there, I checked it. If a damages number's in there, I did the math. That's the difference.

How do you handle confidential client information?

HIPAA-compliant workflows, encrypted file transfer, NDAs standard, and strict matter-scoped processes. Attorney-client privilege is maintained throughout. I never disclose client lists or case details.

What jurisdictions do you cover?

All 50 states. With 20 years of multi-state legal research experience, I'm equipped to handle venue-specific case law, statutes, and procedural requirements across any jurisdiction.

What's your typical turnaround time?

Most demand letters are done in 24–48 hours. Med chronos take 2–5 days depending on how many records you're sending me. If you're in a bind and need something faster, just ask. I've pulled plenty of all-nighters when a case needed it.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

First revision is always included. If it's still not right after that, I'll either fix it or refund you. No questions asked. Twenty years in this business means my reputation matters more than one project fee.
